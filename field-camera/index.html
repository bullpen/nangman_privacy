Privacy Policy for Nangman Field Camera

Effective date: November 8, 2026
Developer: NANGMAN WORKS
App: Nangman Field Camera (com.nangmanworks.fieldphoto)

This policy describes how Nangman Field Camera handles information. It applies to the app version described here and will be updated if data practices change.

Photos, videos, and audio

The app uses your device camera to capture photos and videos and, when you record video with sound, your microphone to record audio. Captured media and watermarks are processed on your device. The developer does not operate a service that receives your photos, videos, audio, or job names as part of normal capture and editing.

Storage and sharing

The app uses a folder you select through Android's Storage Access Framework (SAF) for durable captured-media storage and may keep app-specific copies or caches for gallery display and processing. Job names, capture preferences, selected storage-folder access, and sharing history (including the selected receiving app, when provided by Android) may be stored locally on your device. You control when to share media with another app through Android's sharing interface, including when you export selected files or a ZIP archive. A receiving app or service may process shared media under its own privacy policy.

Important: App-specific cached files and settings may be deleted when you uninstall the app. Media in a user-selected SAF folder is managed separately and is not intended to be deleted merely because the app's cache is removed. However, folder availability and deletion behavior depend on where you saved files and how you manage them. Back up important photos and videos before uninstalling or deleting media.

Information collected by the developer

The current app does not require an account. Based on the reviewed app source, the developer does not receive your name, email address, job names, photos, videos, audio, capture settings, or sharing history on a developer-operated server through the app's normal capture, gallery, and sharing features. These records may be retained locally on your device.

Advertising and third-party services

The current reviewed app build does not include a third-party advertising SDK. If advertising or analytics services are added in a future release, this policy and the Google Play Data safety disclosures will be updated before that release to describe the relevant providers and data practices.

Android, Google Play, and any apps you choose to share content with may separately process information according to their own policies.

Permissions

Camera: to take photos and record videos.
Microphone: to record sound with videos.
Folder access: to save or manage media in a folder you select through Android's system picker.
Internet: the app declares internet access; this does not mean captured media is uploaded to the developer.

Retention and deletion

Captured media, locally cached copies, job names, preferences, and sharing history are stored on your device. You may delete media through the app or your device's file tools where supported. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app may remove app-specific files, settings, and history, while media stored in a separately selected folder may remain. The developer does not maintain a server-side archive of your captured media.

Children's privacy

The app is a general-purpose worksite documentation utility and is not specifically directed to children.

Changes

We may revise this policy when app features or data practices change. The updated effective date will be shown on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@nangmanworks.com.
