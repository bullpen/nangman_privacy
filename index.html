Privacy Policy for Nangman TTS

Effective date: October 12, 2026
Developer: NANGMAN WORKS
App: Nangman TTS (com.nangmanworks.tts)

This Privacy Policy describes how Nangman TTS handles information when you use the app.

Text and audio processing

Nangman TTS converts text you enter into speech using an on-device text-to-speech engine. Text entered for synthesis and generated audio are processed locally on your device. The app does not require an account or upload your text or generated speech to a server for synthesis.

Files and storage

When you choose to export audio, the app saves WAV or MP3 files to the storage location you select on your device. These files remain under your control. You can manage or delete exported files using your device's file-management tools.

Information collected

The current version of Nangman TTS does not require account registration and does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, entered text, or generated audio on the developer's servers. The app does not include an advertising SDK in its current release.

Device permissions

The app may use foreground-service and notification permissions to process user-requested audio exports and display processing status, including while the app is in the background. These permissions are used to support the app's functions, not to collect personal information.

Third-party services

The current app uses an on-device speech synthesis engine. The developer does not use a third-party advertising service in the current version. Android and Google Play may separately process information according to their own policies when you install or use apps through their services.

Data retention and deletion

Text and generated audio used by the app are handled on your device. You can delete exported audio files and remove the app and its local data through your device settings. The developer does not maintain a server-side account or repository of your entered text or generated audio.

Children's privacy

Nangman TTS is a general-purpose text-to-speech utility and is not specifically designed for children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when app functionality or data practices change. Any updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, please contact NANGMAN WORKS through the developer contact information provided on the Nangman TTS Google Play store listing. Developer website: https://nangmanworks.com/.
